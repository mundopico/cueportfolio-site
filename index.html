Methodology

What's actually happening behind "Get your strategy"

Cue's engine blends the same quantitative discipline institutional research desks run on with a language model built to explain it in plain terms, so the rigor doesn't stay locked in a black box. Here's what feeds every recommendation, without pulling back the curtain on the exact scoring logic itself.

01

Valuation & fundamentals

Every holding is screened against trailing and forward fundamentals: earnings, margins, balance-sheet health, and valuation multiples relative to sector peers, not in isolation.

Source: SEC filings (10-K/10-Q), consensus estimates
02

Earnings momentum & quality

Flags whether a company is beating, meeting, or missing estimates, and whether the beat is coming from real revenue growth or one-time items that won't repeat.

Source: Earnings call transcripts, guidance revisions
03

Institutional flow

Reads quarterly ownership filings to see whether large institutions are accumulating or distributing a position: visibility retail investors rarely get on their own.

Source: 13F institutional ownership filings
04

Portfolio fit & diversification

Every idea is scored on what it does to your specific portfolio: closing sector gaps, managing concentration, matching your stated risk tolerance.

Source: Your live holdings, sector & factor models
Quant discipline

A systematic scoring engine, not a hunch

Cue's screening draws on decades-old, peer-reviewed factor-investing research: the value, quality, and momentum factors that underpin most institutional asset management, applied consistently to every holding, every time. No headlines, no emotion, no chasing what's trending.

Language, not just numbers

A reasoning layer that explains itself

A language-reasoning layer sits on top of the scoring engine and turns the underlying signals into a specific, plain-English narrative for your situation, never a generic template. If Cue can't explain a recommendation in a sentence you'd trust from a smart friend, it doesn't surface it.

Daily fundamentals refresh
Prices, estimates, and screens re-run every trading day
Real-time earnings ingestion
New transcripts and guidance are read as they're released
Quarterly institutional-flow updates
Aligned to the 13F filing cadence institutions themselves follow

Cue doesn't predict the market or promise returns: no one honestly can. What it offers is a consistent, documented framework applied identically to every recommendation, with the reasoning shown so you can judge it for yourself instead of taking it on faith.
